# TrueSight research record

Research direction: **Generalization and Reliability of AI-Generated Image Detection**

Current stage: project recovery and research planning. No detector evaluation has been run in this repository.

The original [project README](../README.md) and [demo notebook](../notebook/TrueSight_Demo.ipynb) are preserved as historical artifacts. Their early plans are not the current research schedule. The notebook displays a local image and prints a placeholder message; it does not detect generated images.

## Records

- [Research questions](questions.md): the current question, provisional hypotheses, and methods to investigate.
- [Research log](log.md): dated observations, decisions, and changes in direction.
- [Experiment record](experiments.md): the status of experiments and the evidence required when runs begin.
- [R2 data feasibility](data-feasibility-r2.md): public release channels, terms, training-overlap risks, and acquisition limits.

- [Frontier Methods and Research Gaps](frontier-methods-and-research-gaps.md): publication/resource verification, algorithm analysis, and candidate research questions as of 2026-10-21; no final algorithm selected.

## Keeping the record accurate

Use English for public documentation, code comments, and reports. Keep Chinese discussion notes and personal learning materials outside the public repository.

Add dated log entries when a research decision changes. Keep earlier entries and explain corrections rather than silently replacing the history. Give research questions stable identifiers and link future experiments to them. Record unsupported expectations as hypotheses, not findings.

When work is carried out, identify the human and AI contributions that can be verified. Do not infer student participation from generated code, commit activity, or project ownership. Commit authorship alone is not a research contribution record.

Store small configurations, data manifests, metrics, and reports in Git when appropriate. Record sources, versions, checksums, and access instructions for external data and weights rather than committing large artifacts or redistributing material without checking its terms.
